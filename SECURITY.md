# Security Policy

Sparring is operated by Carnegie Intelligent Technology Limited (Hong Kong).

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@sparringhq.com**. Please include steps to reproduce and the affected endpoint or component. We acknowledge reports within 2 business days and keep you informed until resolution. Please do not test against other customers' tenants or run denial-of-service tests; use a free gateway organisation of your own.

Our full security posture (architecture, encryption, data handling, sub-processors) is published at https://sparringhq.com/security/.

## Scope

- https://app.sparringhq.com (application and `/api/v1`)
- https://sparringhq.com (marketing site, documentation)
- Public code in this GitHub organisation
